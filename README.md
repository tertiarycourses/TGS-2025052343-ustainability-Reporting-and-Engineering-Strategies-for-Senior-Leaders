## About the course

This course equips senior leaders to evaluate engineering solutions, align organisational strategy with sustainable industry practices, and govern sustainability reporting. Case activities connect environmental and societal outcomes with commercial decisions, controls and traceable evidence.

## Learning outcomes

- Review and evaluate engineering solutions to address sustainability challenges.
- Develop and benchmark organisational strategies aligned with sustainable industry trends.
- Ensure organisational compliance with international and local sustainability reporting standards.
- Propose sustainability strategies that balance market competitiveness with environmental and societal benefits.

## Topics covered

- Sustainability and ESG: engineering decisions, emerging risks and policy requirements.
- ESG strategy, stakeholder engagement, scorecards, risk management and benchmarking.
- Sustainability reporting: IFRS S1/S2, GRI, SASB, TCFD, SGX, data controls and assurance.
- Circular economy, sustainable supply chains, carbon reduction and life-cycle assessment.
