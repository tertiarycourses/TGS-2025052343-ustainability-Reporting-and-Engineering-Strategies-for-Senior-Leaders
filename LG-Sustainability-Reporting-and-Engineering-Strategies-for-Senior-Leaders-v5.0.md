# Learner Guide
**Sustainability Reporting and Engineering Strategies for Senior Leaders**  

Course code: `TGS-2025052343`  
Version: 5.0  
Effective date: 01 Oct 2026

## Learning outcomes
### LU1: Foundations of Sustainability and ESG
**Mapping:** K1, K2, A1
Review and endorse engineering solutions using an evidence-based option appraisal.

### LU2: ESG Strategy, Stakeholder Engagement, and Market Positioning
**Mapping:** K3, A3
Develop a material ESG strategy and benchmark it against peers and prevailing trends.

### LU3: Sustainability Reporting, ESG Disclosure, and Compliance
**Mapping:** K4, A2
Build a disclosure-ready evidence chain and compliance crosswalk for IFRS S1/S2, GRI and SGX.

### LU4: Circular Economy, Sustainable Supply Chains, and Carbon Reduction Strategies
**Mapping:** K5, A4
Propose a competitive circular and decarbonisation portfolio with ecological and communal benefits.

## Detailed activity procedures
### Activity 1 — Engineering solution option appraisal
1. Open activities/activity-01-engineering-option-appraisal/mock-data/mock-data.csv and confirm CAPEX, annual savings, reductions, readiness and risk fields.
2. Calculate simple payback = CAPEX ÷ annual savings. Keep the unit in years.
3. Use WORKSHEET.md to normalise each criterion to a 1–5 score and apply the stated weights.
4. Compute weighted score = Σ(score × weight). Check that weights total 100%.
5. Stress-test the recommendation by shifting 10 percentage points from payback to carbon.
6. Complete decision-template.md with endorsed option, conditions, evidence gaps, owner and review date.

**Acceptance checks:** formulas reconcile; assumptions and sources are labelled; thresholds are explicit; results are reproducible; residual uncertainty is stated.

### Activity 2 — ESG materiality and strategic scorecard
1. Open activities/activity-02-materiality-scorecard/mock-data/mock-data.csv and label each item by stakeholder, issue and reliability.
2. Rate stakeholder significance and enterprise impact from 1–5 using WORKSHEET.md.
3. Plot issues on the 5×5 materiality matrix and flag the ≥4/≥4 priority zone.
4. Calculate confidence-adjusted priority = significance × impact × reliability ÷ 5.
5. Build four KPIs with definition, baseline, target, owner, source system, review cadence and tolerance.
6. Record strategic choices in decision-template.md and identify one trade-off requiring approval.

**Acceptance checks:** formulas reconcile; assumptions and sources are labelled; thresholds are explicit; results are reproducible; residual uncertainty is stated.

### Activity 3 — Disclosure control and compliance crosswalk
1. Open activities/activity-03-disclosure-control/mock-data/mock-data.csv and flag missing owners, unsupported estimates and stale records.
2. Trace each metric from source record to calculation, approval and disclosure line.
3. Use references/SOURCES.md to map evidence to IFRS S1/S2, GRI and SGX.
4. Calculate control risk = likelihood × disclosure impact; escalate scores ≥15.
5. Design a control with owner, frequency, input, action, retained evidence and escalation threshold.
6. Complete decision-template.md and state the assertion boundary and limitation.

**Acceptance checks:** formulas reconcile; assumptions and sources are labelled; thresholds are explicit; results are reproducible; residual uncertainty is stated.

### Activity 4 — Circularity and decarbonisation portfolio
1. Open activities/activity-04-circular-decarbonisation/mock-data/mock-data.csv and confirm the reporting boundary.
2. Separate negative annual cost (saving) from positive annual cost.
3. Calculate marginal abatement cost = net annual cost ÷ tCO2e avoided.
4. Compare with the official 2026–2027 Singapore carbon-tax context in references/SOURCES.md.
5. Sequence no-regret, enabling and transformation initiatives with dependencies and evidence gates.
6. Complete decision-template.md with ecological benefit, community impact, commercial value, owners and verification plan.

**Acceptance checks:** formulas reconcile; assumptions and sources are labelled; thresholds are explicit; results are reproducible; residual uncertainty is stated.
